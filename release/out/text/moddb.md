## Summary

Replaces Boso Ta's squeaky voice in X4: Foundations with a calm, deep one. Separate downloads for the German and the English voice track.

## Description

Boso Ta, the Boron scientist at your headquarters, talks a lot, and his original voice is extremely high and shrill in both the German and the English track. This mod gives him a voice you can listen to for hours.

**German (DE):** all 2,324 German lines are spoken anew from the original game texts by a calm male voice, then lowered and warmed. Lip sync is not preserved.

**English (EN):** his 1,750 English lines are the original recordings, processed: lower, slightly alien timbre, mid-range emphasis. Timing and lip sync are unchanged.

## Installation

1. Download the ZIP for your voice track (DE or EN, not both).
2. Extract it into `<X4 installation>\extensions\`. Do not rename the folder.
3. Start X4 and make sure the entry is enabled under Extensions.
