## Description

English voice track only. Replaces Boso Ta's English voice lines with processed copies of the originals: pitched down without slowing him down, a bit of alien shimmer on top, mid-range pushed up so he stays intelligible. Timing and lip sync are unchanged.

Conversations with him no longer get quiet and muffled when you turn away or when he is behind the glass of his tank.

- Use this item if the game speaks English, the German item if it speaks German. Do not subscribe to both.
